# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness across teams, ensuring deployments are planned, communicated, and executed with clear rollback and support plans.

### Core Responsibilities
- Define release scope, timeline, and readiness criteria
- Coordinate go/no-go checks with engineering, QA, and project leadership
- Manage release communications, change windows, and stakeholder notifications
- Ensure rollback plans and incident contacts are prepared before deployment
- Track release outcomes and follow up on post-release action items

### Typical Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers on schedule alignment and risk escalation
- Partner with Developers and Technical Leads on deployment dependencies and rollout steps
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Support/Operations on customer impact and messaging

### Contribution to Project Outcomes and Accountability
- Improves predictability of delivery by enforcing release readiness gates
- Reduces production disruption through proactive planning and rollback preparedness
- Creates clear ownership for release execution decisions and communications

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate stakeholder needs into clear, actionable requirements and acceptance criteria that support reliable planning and implementation.

### Core Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements, constraints, and success criteria
- Refine user stories with clear acceptance criteria and edge-case considerations
- Support backlog grooming with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Identify requirement gaps, dependencies, and assumptions early
- Validate delivered functionality against agreed requirements

### Typical Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers on problem framing, scope clarity, and prioritization
- Support Developers and QA with requirement clarification during implementation and testing
- Collaborate with Project Managers to reduce ambiguity-related delivery risks

### Contribution to Project Outcomes and Accountability
- Reduces rework by improving requirement quality before development starts
- Strengthens accountability by connecting stakeholder intent to deliverable outcomes
- Improves planning confidence through clearer scope and acceptance definitions

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers shape end-to-end user experience by designing flows and interfaces that balance user needs, product goals, and technical feasibility.

### Core Responsibilities
- Create and iterate user flows, wireframes, and interaction designs
- Validate usability assumptions through lightweight research and feedback
- Define UX acceptance criteria and accessibility considerations
- Collaborate with engineering on feasible implementation approaches
- Ensure design consistency across related features and journeys

### Typical Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers and Business Analysts to align user needs with feature scope
- Partner with Developers and Technical Leads during implementation trade-off decisions
- Coordinate with Project Managers on design milestones and dependencies

### Contribution to Project Outcomes and Accountability
- Improves adoption and satisfaction by aligning delivery with real user workflows
- Lowers late-stage rework by validating flows earlier in planning and design
- Clarifies ownership of user-experience quality and accessibility outcomes

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, align implementation decisions across teams, and ensure architecture supports delivery goals at scale.

### Core Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical approach, architecture boundaries, and standards
- Evaluate dependencies, integration risks, and non-functional requirements
- Support estimation and sequencing for technically complex work
- Review design and implementation decisions for maintainability and scalability
- Mentor developers and coordinate technical risk mitigation with QA and PM roles

### Typical Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers on solution design, trade-offs, and code quality expectations
- Partner with Project Managers and Product Managers on scope feasibility and sequencing
- Work with Release Managers and Support/Operations on deployment and reliability considerations

### Contribution to Project Outcomes and Accountability
- Reduces technical risk by making architecture decisions explicit and reviewable
- Improves delivery speed through clearer technical guardrails and dependency planning
- Establishes accountable ownership for system integrity, scalability, and maintainability

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support and Operations Representatives provide production-facing input before release and help monitor, triage, and communicate operational issues after release.

### Core Responsibilities
- Provide production readiness input for monitoring, alerting, and runbooks
- Identify support impacts, operational risks, and handoff requirements
- Monitor post-release health signals and triage incoming incidents
- Coordinate with delivery teams on hotfix priorities and customer-facing updates
- Capture operational feedback to improve future planning and implementation

### Typical Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Release Managers on readiness, rollout communication, and incident paths
- Work with Project Managers and Product Managers on service-level expectations and escalations
- Collaborate with Developers and Technical Leads on observability, diagnostics, and resolution steps

### Contribution to Project Outcomes and Accountability
- Improves resilience by embedding operational considerations into delivery planning
- Reduces time-to-resolution through clearer incident ownership and communication paths
- Strengthens accountability for post-release stability and customer impact handling
